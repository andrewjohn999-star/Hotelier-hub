Who this is for

This policy covers hotelierhub.netlify.app ("the site"). The site is a dashboard tool for hotel operators; it does not currently collect information from hotel guests.

What we store, and why

  • Your selected hotel and postcode — stored in a cookie on your device so the site remembers your choice on return visits. Not sent to us or any third party; it stays on your device.
  • Your theme preference — stored in your browser's local storage, for the same reason.
  • Your cookie consent choice — stored in a cookie so we don't ask again every visit.

We don't currently run analytics or advertising on this site. The cookie banner includes an "Analytics" category for future use, but nothing is collected under it today.

Postcode lookups

When you enter a postcode, it's sent to two external services to find nearby hotels: postcodes.io (to convert it to a location) and OpenStreetMap's Overpass API (to find hotels near that location). Neither service is told who you are — just the postcode itself, momentarily, to return results.

News search

Trade and local news headlines are retrieved via Google's Custom Search API. Your postcode area may be included in a local news search query sent to Google for this purpose.

Cookies used on this site

  • Necessary — your selected hotel, postcode, and cookie consent choice. These support features you've directly asked for and are used regardless of consent choice, in line with ICO guidance on strictly necessary cookies.
  • Analytics — not currently in use. If added later, this section will be updated and the consent banner will reflect it.

Your choices

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the banner, clear cookies via your browser settings, or use the "Change" option next to your saved hotel to update or remove it.
